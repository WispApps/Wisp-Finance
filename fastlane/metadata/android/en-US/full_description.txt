Wisp Finance is an open-source personal finance app for Android.

Track expenses, plan budgets, manage subscriptions and follow savings goals. Keep records of loans, debts, investments and crypto, with built-in calculators and spending insights.

Available in 10 languages, with the interface language and currency chosen independently.

Export encrypted backups and optionally sync them through your own Nextcloud server (HTTPS WebDAV). Restoring a backup requires both the file and its recovery phrase, so save them before clearing app data or uninstalling.

Free software under GPL-3.0-or-later.
